// THINGS THAT BROKE, AND WHAT I DID

A retried webhook paid people twice

Paystack retries on timeout. My referral payout handler wasn't idempotent, so a single payment credited an ambassador twice. Fixed with a $ne-filtered conditional updateOne so the write only lands once — and learned the hard way why you never pair that filter with upsert: true.

A slow dashboard that wasn't a slow query

Quick-stats took ~4s. Profiling showed 1ms of actual database work behind 111ms of round-trip latency, 43 times over. Collapsed it into a single $group with conditional accumulators — 43 round trips to 3. I also measured $facet, which made it worse: it re-scans per branch.

74% of image bytes were being wasted

Product images were served untransformed from Cloudinary. Adding a transformation helper at the delivery layer cut a real storefront from 1,852KB to 467KB. It beat next/image here, and I kept the LCP image eager.
